The Problem

Lost Your Only Car Key?

Losing your only set of car keys usually means a call to the dealership, a tow to their lot, and a multi-day wait. A+ Lock & Service skips all of that — our mobile unit carries the equipment to cut and program a new key or smart fob for most makes and models, on-site, using your vehicle's information.

Whether it's a basic transponder key or a modern smart fob, we can usually get you a working replacement without ever needing the original.
